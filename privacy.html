Privacy Policy

Verdant (“we”, “our”, or “the Mini App”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we process when you use the Verdant Telegram Mini App.

1. Information we collect

  • Telegram profile data — user ID, first name, last name, username, and profile photo provided by the Telegram WebApp SDK when you open the Mini App.
  • App activity — tasks completed, check-ins, balance, streak, referrals, and withdrawal requests stored in our Firebase database.
  • Proof submissions — usernames, links or short notes you send for tasks that need review. We never ask for passwords.
  • Technical data — anonymous authentication identifiers required to keep your progress linked across sessions.

2. How we use information

  • To operate the earning tasks, check-in, wallet, and referral features.
  • To show your balance, streak, and leaderboard ranking.
  • To process withdrawal requests and prevent abuse.

3. Storage & security

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Authentication + Firestore). Access is restricted by Firebase security rules. We do not sell your personal data.

4. Third parties

We use Telegram (for the Mini App environment) and Google Firebase (for auth and database). Their own privacy policies also apply.

5. Your choices

You can stop using the Mini App at any time. For data deletion requests, contact support through the Help section in Profile.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent via the Help & Support option in your Profile.
